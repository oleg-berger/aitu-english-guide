Week 1. How Technology Changed Education

Материалы: F02, F03, F05; F03: Vocabulary Activation, Speaking Discussion, AI Classroom 2030; F05: Set A/B, определения и коллокации.

Вы обсуждали, как технологии меняют обучение: доступ к материалам, онлайн-взаимодействие, обратная связь и индивидуальную траекторию. Нужно оценивать пользу для обучения, а не только удобство. Риски: зависимость от AI, ошибки, недостаток собственных навыков, приватность, cheating, неравный доступ к устройствам и интернету, меньше личного общения.

1. How has technology changed education?

Technology gives students access to digital resources, virtual classrooms and faster feedback. It can make learning more flexible, but students still need to evaluate information and practise their own skills.

Назовите конкретные изменения и хотя бы один риск.

2. What is the difference between adaptive and personalized learning?

Adaptive learning changes tasks according to performance. Personalized learning is tailored more broadly to a learner’s needs, goals or pace.

Adaptive — механизм изменения по результатам; personalized — индивидуальная направленность.

3. Will AI replace human teachers?

AI can support explanations and feedback, but teachers still guide learning, support students and judge context. I would use AI as an assistant while keeping human oversight.

Это пример аргументированной позиции из обсуждения, а не установленный прогноз.

4. Design an AI classroom and give three rules.

Our classroom would use an AI tutor and adaptive exercises. Students must verify AI answers, disclose AI assistance and protect personal data. The teacher would check understanding and support students.

Ответ покрывает инструменты, роли, три правила; добавьте риск и решение при длинном ответе.

Week 1. Academic Integrity and Misconduct

Материалы: F02, F04; F04: слайды 4–8, 9–13; F02: Course Policy и Assignment 1.

Academic integrity — честность, доверие, справедливость, уважение и ответственность в учебной работе. Важно сохранять собственный вклад, признавать чужие идеи и отвечать за представленную работу. AI-помощь оценивается по назначению, объёму, раскрытию использования и правилам конкретного задания.

5. How is collusion different from legitimate collaboration?

Collaboration is permitted by the task. Collusion is unauthorised cooperation on work that is supposed to show independent performance.

Критерий — разрешение и назначение задания.

6. A student creates fake survey responses with AI. What is the violation?

It is fabricating information because invented data are presented as genuine research results.

AI-инструмент не меняет тип нарушения.

7. A student practises with AI before an exam but uses only permitted materials during it. Is this exam cheating?

In the lesson’s scenario, this is ethical and responsible preparation, not unauthorised help during the exam.

Оцениваем условия именно приведённого сценария.

8. How can students avoid plagiarism?

They should acknowledge borrowed ideas, cite sources, quote exact wording correctly and paraphrase accurately in their own words. They should also disclose AI use as required.

Paraphrase всё равно требует ссылки.

9. What is the difference between an in-text citation and a reference?

An in-text citation briefly identifies the source within the text. A reference gives fuller publication details so the reader can find it.

Короткое указание рядом с идеей и полные данные в списке.

10. Does a paraphrase need a citation?

Yes. The wording is yours, but the idea comes from another source.

Изменение формулировки не делает чужую идею вашей.

11. Do private emails go in the reference list in the lesson’s APA exercise?

No. Private emails are treated as personal communications and cited in the text only.

Речь о недоступных читателю личных сообщениях; не о публичной публикации.

12. Why should a student check a citation generator’s output?

A generator can help with formatting, but the student must verify the authors, date, title and source details. Students remain responsible for their references.

Генератор облегчает оформление; он не доказывает существование источника.

13. What is a research gap?

A research gap is an important issue that existing research has not adequately answered. A study may aim to fill that gap.

Не просто тема статьи, а недостающий ответ в исследованиях.

14. What is the difference between Results and Discussion?

Results report what was found. Discussion explains what those findings mean and evaluates their implications.

Факт результата и его интерпретация.

15. What should you describe in Methodology?

I should describe the research design, participants or data, data collection and analysis tools. If the study analyses public posts, I should say that instead of inventing recruited participants.

Важно не превращать tweets автоматически в «участников эксперимента».

16. How do you use evidence in an academic answer?

I state the claim, give a relevant statistic or example from the source, and explain how it supports the claim.

Не просто привести число: объяснить его связь с утверждением.

17. What did the authors analyse, and what were the main results?

They analysed 3,870 filtered tweets about ChatGPT. VADER classified 2,013 as positive, 1,053 as neutral and 804 as negative. The selected posts were generally favourable, but the study also discussed risks for education.

Укажите данные и метод; не называйте tweets экспериментом со студентами.

18. Does positive sentiment prove that ChatGPT improves learning?

No. Positive sentiment shows attitudes in the selected posts. It does not directly measure learning outcomes or prove a causal improvement.

Мнение пользователей и доказательство результата обучения — разные виды данных.

19. Why are AI-detection results insufficient on their own?

Detection tools can misclassify human writing and may struggle with short texts. Their output should be checked alongside other evidence.

Не обещайте 100% распознавание AI.

20. How can universities use ChatGPT responsibly?

They can set clear rules, require disclosure, verify outputs and design tasks that show students’ own understanding. AI should support learning rather than replace students’ thinking.

Перечислите конкретные меры.

Week 3. How False Information Spreads

Материалы: F17, F18, F19, F20; Listening Parts 1–2 и полный транскрипт; circular reporting, source checking.

Circular reporting возникает, когда разные публикации ссылаются друг на друга, а в основе остаётся одна непроверенная история. Повторение создаёт видимость независимого подтверждения.
